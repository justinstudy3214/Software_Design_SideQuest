How your feed works

Each genre you tap is worth 3 points. Each game you like adds 1 point to its genres, and each game you mark "Not for me" takes 1 point away. Games are ranked by those points plus how much they're trending, with a small daily shuffle so the list changes from one day to the next. Two games from genres you haven't tried are mixed in so you keep finding new things. Your choices are saved to your account, so they follow you when you log in again.

Sidequest is a prototype. The catalog is 27 sample games with descriptions written for this demo, and the trending numbers are sample data. To add or change games, use the admin dashboard or edit data/games.json and restart the server.
